Briefing

Welcome to the Devil Hunter Field Manual. This document serves as a comprehensive briefing for all incoming Public Safety Devil Hunter recruits. Within these pages you will find everything you need to know about the world you are now a part of the devils that threaten humanity, the fiends that walk among us, the organizations that fight back, and the grey areas that make this job anything but simple. Read carefully. Your life may depend on it.

Power System & Contracts

In order for a human to stand against a Devil, the best course of action is to form a Contract with another Devil. Every contract between a human and a devil operates on a strict quid pro quo where something is given and something is taken. No contract comes without a price. With a contract, a human can use a part of Devil's power and use it in combat against another Devil. Take Denji for example, Pochita merged with Denji for one condition:

"Here's a new contract for you. This time, you're getting my heart. In exchange, show me your dreams. That's all I ask of you."
— Pochita, Chainsaw Man Ep. 1, IMDB
Essentially Pochita fuses his heart with Denji, then becomes a Chainsaw Hybrid. Another example would be Aki Hayakawa. Exchanging skin off his arms for the power of the Fox Devil which is Public Safety's most used contracted Devil that's been shown. Every contract is a gamble where the stronger the Devil, the steeper the price. In this world, power never comes free.

Public Safety

Public Safety Devil Hunters are government employed hunters tasked with eliminating Devils that pose a threat to civilians. Organized into special divisions, these hunters work in teams and are often contracted with Devils themselves to level the playing field. Japan's Public Safety Division is one of the most prominent in the world, housing some of the strongest Devil Hunters alive. It is not a glamorous job as the mortality rate is high and the pay is modest. Most hunters don't last long. Those that do are either incredibly skilled, incredibly lucky, or both.

"The hunters that devils fear are the ones with a couple of screws loose."
— Kishibe, Chainsaw Man Ep. 7, MagicalQuote

Devils

Devils are mysterious beings born from human fear. The more feared a concept is, the stronger its Devil becomes. They exist in both the human world and Hell, and when killed in one realm they simply revive in the other which makes them nearly impossible to permanently get rid of. Devils range wildly in power, from weak low-tier Devils that a rookie hunter could handle, all the way up to the Primal Fears where Devils so deeply rooted in human instinct that they have never once been defeated. All Devils have one thing in common: they are drawn to blood and chaos.

Fiends

When a Devil takes over a human corpse, the result is a Fiend. Unlike a full Devil, Fiends are bound to their human body and cannot transform freely, though they retain the Devil's power and personality. They are distinguishable by the deformed appearance of their head, which takes on characteristics of the Devil inhabiting them. Fiends are considered easier to deal with than full Devils since they are limited by their human host. Public Safety even employs several Fiends as Devil Hunters, including the Blood Fiend, Power and the Violence Fiend.

Hybrids

Hybrids are the rarest and most powerful result of a Devil merging with a living human rather than a corpse. Unlike Fiends, Hybrids retain their human consciousness and can transform into a Devil form at will, typically by pulling a trigger or activating some mechanism on their body. They also possess extraordinary regeneration, making them incredibly difficult to kill. Known hybrids include Denji, the Chainsaw Man hybrid, Reze the Bomb Devil hybrid, and Katana Man. Their existence makes them highly sought after by governments and organizations worldwide.
